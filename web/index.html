Put-call parity

C − P + K·e−rT − S, by strike
A large residual reads as a stale, illiquid, or asynchronous print -- one leg's last trade may be minutes away from the other's -- never as a live arbitrage, since neither price is a firm quote. In this dataset almost every strike pair exceeds the ±15 pt tolerance: with no bid/ask and two independent last-traded prints, that is the expected result, not a broken filter -- it's the reason "Parity-valid only" above will show few or no contracts for most expiries.
